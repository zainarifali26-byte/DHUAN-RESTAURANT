The room

Fourteen seats around the hearth.

Chef Mara Ellison cooks every course within arm's reach of the counter. There is no pass and no back kitchen: what you watch being made is what arrives in front of you, minutes later.

Seafood comes from Tomales Bay and Hokkaido, produce from farms an hour north, and the fire is fed with almond wood from the Central Valley.
